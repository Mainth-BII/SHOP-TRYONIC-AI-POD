## 1. Project Overview
POD T-Shirt Platform is an AI-powered e-commerce system allowing users to generate artwork, design custom apparel, and manage orders. Phase 1 focuses on a Manual Fulfillment MVP with core AI and E-commerce features.

## 2. Critical Specification Analysis

### 2.1 Core Modules & Logic
- **AI Artwork Engine (F01/F04):** 
    - Input: Prompt (VN/EN) + Style.
    - Output: 2-4 variations (512px).
    - Credits: -5 for Gen, -3 for Upscale (≥2048px).
    - Quota: 10/day default.
- **Order & Payments (F10/F11):** 
    - Cancelation: Allowed at any stage (BR-ORD-01).
    - Expiry: 24h for unpaid orders (BR-ORD-04).
    - Logic: VNPay (Auto) vs Bank Transfer (Manual).
- **Guest Mode & Migration (F08b/Flow 3):** 
    - Data: localStorage (7-day expiry).
    - Migration: Cart + Designs synced to account after login.
- **Credits Economy (F14/Flow 6):** 
    - Earn: +5 (Signup), +10 (Delivery), +2 (Review).
    - Constraints: Non-negative balance, no expiry (Phase 1).
- **Smart Fit-Size (BR-FIT):** 
    - BMI-based mapping from Height/Weight/Gender.
    - Visual: Confidence % display.

## 3. Risk Assessment & Testing Strategies
- **Connectivity Risk:** AI Generator or Payment Gateway timeouts.
- **Data Integrity:** Guest-to-User migration conflicts.
- **Security:** OAuth vulnerabilities and Content Policy (copyright/offensive images).
- **Vietnamese Language:** Proper handling of UTF-8 and auto-translation for prompts.

## 4. Test Case Scope (100+ Scenarios)
- **AI Modules:** Focus on Quota, Quality, and Credits deduction.
- **Guest Flow:** Validate localStorage sync and migration logic.
- **Payment:** Test both VNPay successes/failures and Manual Bank Transfer verification.
- **Admin:** Validate User Management actions and CRM-style detail views.
- **Mobile Integrity:** Ensure Editor and Mockups are responsive and functional on small viewports.
